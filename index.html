Running both systems — the rule

This terminal is signal-only, like your indicator: it never places a trade. Smart Grid Hunter stays on its M1 chart, the Scalp Panel on M5. If this desk and your MT5 panel disagree, the safe read is WAIT — two systems fighting is information, not a trade.

How the desk decides

1. Wake & scan — fetch the last 200 closed candles for the chosen timeframe (15m / 1h / 4h).
2. Council votes — Technical (EMA 21/55 trend + 20-bar breakout / range-edge position), Momentum (MACD + price vs EMA), Sentiment (RSI zone), Risk (ATR as % of price — too wild = no trade).
3. Consensus — at least 3 of 4 must agree the same way, and Risk must not veto. Otherwise the signal is WAIT.
4. Plan — Entry = last close. Stop = 1.0 × ATR away. Targets at 1.0R / 1.8R / 2.6R — the same distances as your SP Gold Scalp Panel.
5. H1 bias — computed on 1h candles (EMA 21/55 + ADX-style trend strength) and shown at the top; a scan against the H1 bias gets its conviction cut.

What this is not: it is not a broker, it holds no money, and it cannot execute on MT5 — there is no auto-trade bridge in v1 on purpose. It is also not Zoqira: same kind of desk, your own rules, your own honest record.
